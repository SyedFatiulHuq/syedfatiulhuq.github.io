# title | description
How accessibility materializes in practice | Studies of what developers and users say and do about accessibility, from Twitter conversations (#A11ydev, CHI 2023) to issue reports in open source projects.
Automated tools' alignment with disabled experiences | User studies with blind testers to characterize accessibility issues and evaluate detection tools. A mixed-methods study (CHI 2026) yielded 28 screen reader issue types in five categories, and the MCAG mobile accessibility guidelines.
Streamlining user feedback as accessibility reports | Reca11 turns recorded test-session transcripts from disabled testers into developer-ready reports (ICSE 2025 Distinguished Paper; 98% recall, 85% precision). The approach inspired a deployed feature on Fable's platform.
Are LLMs good at accessibility? | GenA11y uses LLMs to detect context-dependent web accessibility issues (FSE 2025). A new empirical study evaluates the accessibility of 90 websites generated by LLM coding agents.
